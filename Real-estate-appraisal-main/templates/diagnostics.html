Model Performance ≠ Individual Prediction

The metrics below describe how the model performs across many test houses. They do not represent the exact error of any specific house you enter in the Predict page. Each individual prediction may differ from the actual price by more or less than the average MAE.

⚠️ Important Limitations

  • EstateAI is trained on historical housing data from Ames, Iowa. It may not generalise to other cities or time periods.
  • The model uses 19 selected features. Properties with highly unusual combinations may produce larger errors.
  • Model estimates should not replace a professional property appraisal for financial or legal purposes.
  • Ridge Regression assumes a linear relationship between features and price. Non-linear dynamics may not be fully captured.
